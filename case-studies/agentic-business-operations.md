# Agentic Business Operations

## Business Problem
Business owners were absorbing repetitive research, proposal, follow-up, onboarding, QA, and operational work that created unnecessary administrative load and slowed execution.

## Discovery
The core issue was not a lack of AI tools. It was fragmented work, unclear ownership, and too much manual coordination across repeatable tasks.

## Solution
I designed a specialized AI-agent operating model with:

- Defined agent roles
- Task routing
- Shared context
- Workflow state
- Prompt libraries
- QA gates
- MCP-connected tools
- Human approval controls
- Draft-then-approve operating patterns

The goal was to automate repeatable work while keeping higher-consequence decisions visible and auditable.

## Governance
The system follows a simple principle: control matches consequence. Agents can assist, route, research, draft, and execute lower-risk actions within defined boundaries, while sensitive or consequential actions remain subject to human approval.

## Outcome
The model reduced manual administrative load, accelerated repeatable operating work, and created a scalable path for AI adoption without simply adding headcount.
