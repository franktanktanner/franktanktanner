# AI Voice Recruiting Workflow

## Business Problem
Manual first-round applicant screening slowed response times and created inconsistent candidate evaluation. Hiring teams were spending time on repetitive early-stage screening while candidates waited for first contact.

## Discovery
The key requirement was speed without giving an autonomous system final hiring authority. The workflow needed to improve time-to-contact and consistency while keeping managers in control of employment decisions.

## Solution
I designed a multi-turn AI voice workflow using Retell with:

- Structured interview prompts
- Weighted scoring
- Post-call processing
- Structured outputs
- Manager dashboard output
- QA checks
- Explicit human decision controls

## Governance
The system follows an agents-act, humans-decide pattern. AI handles the repetitive first-contact and structured evaluation workflow, while managers retain final hiring authority.

## Outcome
Applicants can be contacted in under a minute and managers receive standardized evaluation data without running every first screen manually.
