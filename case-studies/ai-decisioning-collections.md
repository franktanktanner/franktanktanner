# AI Decisioning & Collections Transformation

## Business Problem
A receivables operation was recovering 1.2% on a $38M portfolio. Outreach was fragmented across channels, next-action logic was inconsistent, and the operating model did not scale cleanly.

## Discovery
The opportunity was not simply to add a chatbot or automate messages. The workflow required coordinated decisioning across customer context, communication channels, payments, analytics, and human review.

## Solution
I designed a phased AI decisioning and workflow transformation spanning:

- Voice AI
- SMS and email
- Payment workflows
- Recovery analytics
- Structured decision logic
- Human approval controls
- Governance and operational review

The implementation was designed around measurable recovery improvement rather than AI usage as the goal.

## Governance
Higher-consequence actions retained explicit human oversight. Automation boundaries, approval points, and workflow controls were defined before scaling.

## Outcome
Recovery increased from **1.2% to 4.7%** on the $38M portfolio.

That represented an estimated **$1.3M in added annual recoveries** and converted the consulting engagement into a **$157K Year-1 SaaS contract**.
